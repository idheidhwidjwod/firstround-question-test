Junior Data Analyst, Operations (80–100%)
Alpenrail Logistics SA (fictional company, for practice) · Lausanne · Hybrid

About us
Alpenrail Logistics moves freight by rail between Swiss industrial sites and the main European corridors. Our operations team plans about 400 trains a week and wants to make better use of its data.

Your role
- Build and maintain dashboards on on-time performance, wagon use and delays (Power BI).
- Write SQL queries on our operations database and automate weekly reports in Python.
- Analyse the causes of delays with the planning team and propose improvements.
- Present your results to operations managers, in French and in English.

Your profile
- Master's degree in engineering, data science, management or a related field. Recent graduates are welcome.
- Good SQL and Python (pandas); experience with Power BI or Tableau.
- You can explain numbers to people who are not specialists.
- French and English; German is a plus.
- Interest in transport, logistics or sustainability.

We offer
A first job with real responsibility, mentoring by a senior analyst, and a team that ships improvements every month.
